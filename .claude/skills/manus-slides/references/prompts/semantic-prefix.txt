ONE finished 16:9 presentation slide, whole-slide semantic exec-sketch visualization.
Not an isolated illustration pasted beside bullets; not a collage of slides; no physical room,
whiteboard, chalk texture, generic 3D or decorative people. The diagram IS the explanation.
Use the exact palette and supplied brand typography. Crisp professional sans-serif main text;
slightly imperfect, confident hand-drawn contours, arrows, underlines and objects. All strokes
must have narrative purpose. The accent marks only the active idea, not every object.
Use generous whitespace, strong hierarchy and a single visual argument. Minimum side margins
6%; top/bottom safe margins 8%. No boxes labelled 'logo here', no fake logos or footer.
Keep requested asset reservations visually quiet with continuous background, not placeholder boxes.
Render the supplied heading and other text EXACTLY ONCE. No invented words or numbers.
For Russian use correct Cyrillic, no Latin lookalikes, duplicated words, spelling mistakes or truncation.
Use supplied reference images ONLY for style, never copy their facts or identities.
Optional authentic logos, portraits, QR and real screenshots are composed separately after generation.
Text must read as typography, not shaky handwriting. Keep diagram labels short and readable.
Draw meaningful coherent objects large enough to understand in a recorded course video.
ACCENT BUDGET. The accent color marks at most two things: ONE key phrase (in the heading or the
takeaway, not both) and the ONE connector or object that carries the active idea. Everything else
is ink only. At most ONE small burst of 2-3 emphasis rays on the whole slide, at the active idea;
no rays around the heading, frames, captions or corners. Section captions are plain ink text
without highlighter bars. On a light canvas the accent is a filled shape with an ink outline or a
highlighter behind ink text, never a thin accent line on paper.
TYPE HIERARCHY. Three sizes plus a footnote: heading; the instruction or takeaway at about half
the heading size; object labels at about a third; an optional footnote at the object-label size
in the secondary color with no highlight.
DRAWING LANGUAGE. Outline-first line drawings; no solid white fills and no filled shape larger
than a label, except the ONE active object. Repeated items share an identical structure. No stock
pictograms: no mountain-and-sun image placeholders, no random squares, circles or triangles as
bullets, no decorative braces. Equal line weights on light and dark slides: main contours about
6 px at 1920 px width, details about 3 px, connectors and arrows about 10 px.
